# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Casual Korean BBQ diners who want a quick, entertaining way to track a shared
all-you-can-eat meal, see the assumptions behind its estimated value, and keep
their own local records without an account or setup ceremony.

## Product Purpose

AYCE Damage Calculator turns a playful question — did we beat the buffet? —
into a transparent, local-first calculation. It should be immediately usable
with the built-in Australian KBBQ catalogue while allowing diners to record
their own pricing and menu assumptions when they need them.

## Brand Personality

Playful, rigorous, local-first. The voice can be wry about the meal, but the
maths, data boundaries and user control must always be clear and dependable.

## Anti-references

Do not turn the calculator into a generic SaaS settings dashboard, a cloud
service, or a setup wizard. Avoid fake restaurant data, implied live exchange
rates, and decorative UI that competes with the task at hand.

## Design Principles

- Keep the default meal journey immediate; configuration is an enhancement, not
  a prerequisite.
- State monetary assumptions plainly and never imply precision the app does not
  have.
- Keep local data visibly local and make destructive actions deliberate.
- Reuse one meal model everywhere so the builder, live mode, history and shared
  reports agree.
- Let the existing dark grill aesthetic support reading and action rather than
  becoming visual noise.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Maintain keyboard-operable controls, visible focus, labelled forms, AA
contrast, responsive use from 320 px upwards, and reduced-motion support.
Important updates are announced without overwhelming assistive technology.
